Institutional privacy notice

Please read before registering. ShikshaSetu is an institution-managed learning workspace. Your institution can administer your account and the academic records you submit. Chatbot monitoring is separately disclosed and requires your consent to use the institutional companion.

What information is stored

Depending on the features you use, ShikshaSetu stores your account details, institution membership, academic records, progress check-ins, timestamps, chatbot messages and replies, topic estimates, and relevant security and audit events. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes, not as readable passwords.

What your institution administrators can see

Authorised administrators in your own institution can manage your membership and academic records, see progress and chatbot usage summaries, and review retained chatbot conversation records when necessary for student support or institutional administration. Access is restricted by institutional role, and administrator review events are recorded in audit logs. One institution's administrators cannot access another institution's private records through the portal.

Chatbot monitoring and consent

Chatbot messages may be stored with topic and emotional-tone estimates so the service can provide supportive responses and your institution can review relevant usage. Topic and emotional-tone estimates are transparent keyword-based cues, not psychological diagnoses. The activity grouping model uses usage metadata such as interaction counts, active days, topic breadth and average message length; it does not use the content of your messages or sentiment labels as ML features. Activity groups do not measure ability, wellbeing or risk.

You may withdraw chatbot monitoring consent from your account profile. Withdrawing consent pauses further use of the institutional chatbot until consent is enabled again. It does not instantly erase conversations already retained; those records follow the configured deletion schedule and can also be handled through your institution's applicable data request process.

Retention and security

Chat records are automatically deleted after the institution's configured retention period (90 days by default; the application permits a setting between 7 and 365 days). Academic and account records follow institutional retention and legal requirements. Sessions expire, administrators have role-limited access, and sensitive review actions are audit logged. Institutions should deploy ShikshaSetu over HTTPS, protect database backups, configure database credentials and encryption at rest, and publish their own contact and retention policies before production use.

Student support and important limitations

The chatbot provides educational and supportive guidance; it is not a counsellor, emergency service, medical service or substitute for a trusted human. AI-generated analytics are decision-support information and must not be used as the sole basis for disciplinary, academic or wellbeing decisions. Contact a teacher, mentor, counsellor or campus support team when human help is needed. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Your choices and questions

You can review your profile and chatbot consent within the student dashboard after your institution approves your account. For questions, corrections or deletion requests, contact your institution administrator, who is responsible for responding under the institution's applicable policies and law.
